ATTENDANCE RULES

1. Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for semester examinations.

2. Calculation of Attendance
Attendance is calculated based on the total number of classes conducted for each course.

3. Shortage of Attendance
Students with attendance below 75% may not be permitted to appear for the examinations.

4. Medical Leave
Attendance shortage due to medical reasons may be considered if supported by valid medical certificates and approved by the college authorities.

5. Duty Leave
Students representing the institution in official events (sports, cultural, technical) may be granted duty leave, which will be considered as present.

6. Attendance Monitoring
Attendance records are updated regularly and can be accessed through the student portal.

7. Warning System
Students falling below 75% attendance will receive warnings and must take corrective measures.

8. Final Decision
The decision of the academic committee regarding attendance eligibility will be final and binding.

9. Exceptional Cases
Special consideration may be given in exceptional cases subject to approval by the principal or academic head.
